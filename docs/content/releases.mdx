## How to update

Install the new version **over** the old one. The in-app updater does this: **Settings → Check for updates**, then install.

<Danger title="Never uninstall first">
  Uninstalling deletes your playlists, liked songs and history, and Android cannot get them back.

  Your **downloads** are safe either way. They are in your own music folder.
</Danger>

See **[Updates](/reference/updates)**.

## Version numbers

Versions look like `v1.2.9`. The number in **Settings → App version** always matches the release. Versions are compared as numbers, so `v1.0.10` is newer than `v1.0.9`.

## Reporting a problem

**[Open an issue](https://github.com/subh-775/Music_Player/issues)** with:

- What you did, and what happened instead.
- Your Android version and phone model.
- The version from **Settings → App version**.
