# Fair Use

Fix_Music is a personal project, built to learn how a music client is put together. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Spotify, JioSaavn, SoundCloud or YouTube.

## What the app actually does

It searches three **public** catalogues, resolves the stream those services already serve to a browser, and plays it. It stores nothing centrally and it hosts no music.

That is worth stating precisely, because it is the difference between a client and a distribution service. This is a client.

## What that does not make it

It does not make everything you can reach with it yours, and it does not make every use of it legal where you live. Copyright is territorial and the terms of the services it talks to are their own.

Use it for material you have the right to use.

## Support the people who made the music

This one matters more than the paragraph above it.

Streaming royalties are already thin. Downloading a song instead of streaming it removes even that. If you listen to an artist enough to want their music on your phone permanently:

- **Buy the record.** Bandcamp pays artists a far larger share than any streaming service.
- **Go to a show.** Live income is what most working musicians actually live on.
- **Buy the merch** at that show, not from a reseller.
- **Keep a subscription** to a service that pays, if you can afford one.

An app that makes music easy to get is only defensible if the people who made it still get paid. That is on the person listening, not on the software.

## No warranty

The app is provided as-is. It talks to services that change without notice, and when one of them changes, search breaks until an update ships. There is no uptime commitment and there is nobody on call.
