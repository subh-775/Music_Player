# Fair Use

Relaxify is a personal project, made to learn how a music app works. It is not connected to, or approved by, Spotify, JioSaavn, SoundCloud or YouTube.

## What the app does

It searches three **public** catalogues, gets the same stream those services send to a web browser, and plays it. It stores nothing on a server and hosts no music.

## What that does not mean

It does not make everything you can reach with it yours. It may not make every use legal where you live. Copyright laws differ by country, and each service has its own terms.

Use it only for music you have the right to use.

## Support the artists

Streaming pays artists very little. Downloading pays them nothing. If you listen to an artist enough to keep their music:

- **Buy the music.** Bandcamp gives artists a much bigger share than streaming.
- **Go to a show.** Live shows are how most musicians earn a living.
- **Buy merch** at the show.
- **Pay for a streaming service** that pays artists, if you can.

## No warranty

The app is provided as is. The services it uses can change without notice. When one does, search may stop working until an update is out.

## Take-down requests

If you represent a rights holder and think this project breaks your rights, **[open an issue](https://github.com/subh-775/Music_Player/issues)**.
