# Quick Start

First search to first download, in about five minutes. Nothing here needs to be configured first.

## Play something

1. Tap **Search** in the bottom bar.
2. Type a song and artist. Suggestions appear as you type; press search or pick one.
3. Tap any row. It starts playing, and the **mini player** rises above the bottom bar.

The row's badge says which source it came from — JioSaavn, SoundCloud or YouTube — and its bitrate. If a track fails to start, the app skips it and says which one and why, rather than sitting there.

## Open the full player

Tap the mini player. Now try, in this order:

- **Swipe the artwork left.** Next song. The incoming title travels with the cover.
- **Double-tap the right half.** Forward ten seconds. Do it again: twenty.
- **Pull up** anywhere along the bottom row, or tap the queue glyph on its right. That is the queue.
- **Drag down** from the artwork or the top strip. The player goes away and the mini player is back.

Those four are most of the app. **[Gestures](/guide/gestures)** has the rest.

<Tip>
  The three glyphs to the right of the title are **add to playlist**, **like**, and **download**. Like is what fills your Liked Songs; download is what makes the song work with no connection.
</Tip>

## Keep it playing

Autoplay is on by default. When the queue runs out, the app builds a radio from what you have been listening to and keeps going rather than stopping dead.

To queue something deliberately, **long-press any song row** and choose *Play next* or *Add to queue*.

## Download for offline

Tap the **⭳** glyph in the player, or long-press a row and choose Download.

The first time, Android asks for storage permission. After that:

- The glyph becomes a **green tick** the moment the file is on disk.
- The song plays with no connection at all, and carries an **Offline** badge.
- The file is a real tagged audio file in your music folder — it plays in any other app.

See **[Downloads & Offline](/guide/downloads)**.

## Make it sound the way you want

Open the drawer — **swipe in from the left edge of Home**, or tap the hamburger.

- **Equalizer** — eight bands and eleven presets. It needs something playing to attach to; start a song first.
- **Your sound** — normalize volume and the audio-effect switches.
- **Settings → Playback** — crossfade, normalize volume, streaming quality.

The **sleep timer** is not in the drawer. It is the timer glyph at the bottom left of the full player, next to the thing it stops.

<Note>
  Dragging any equalizer band puts you in **Custom**, seeded from whatever curve is on screen. A preset is a starting point, not a cage.
</Note>

## Import what you already have

Paste a public Spotify playlist or album link into the search box. Every track is matched against the three sources and the result is saved as an ordinary playlist you can edit.

See **[Import from Spotify](/guide/spotify-import)**.

## The five-minute checklist

- [x] Played a song from search
- [x] Swiped the artwork to change track
- [x] Opened the queue from the player's bottom row
- [x] Downloaded one song and seen the tick
- [x] Found the drawer from the left edge

That is the app. Everything after this is detail.
