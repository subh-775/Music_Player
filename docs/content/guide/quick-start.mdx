# Quick Start

From your first search to your first download in about five minutes. You do not need to set anything up first.

## Play a song

1. Tap **Search** in the bottom bar.
2. Type a song and artist. Pick a suggestion or press search.
3. Tap a row. The song starts and the **mini player** appears above the bottom bar.

Each row shows its source: JioSaavn, SoundCloud or YouTube. If a song cannot start, the app skips it and tells you which one.

## Open the full player

Tap the mini player, then try these:

- **Swipe the cover left** for the next song.
- **Double-tap the right half** to jump forward 10 seconds. Tap again for 20.
- **Pull up** on the bottom row, or tap the queue button on its right, to see the queue.
- **Drag down** from the cover or the top strip to close the player.

**[Gestures](/guide/gestures)** has the rest.

<Tip>
  The two buttons to the right of the title are **download** and **+**. Press **+** once to save the song to Liked Songs. Press it again to add it to playlists. Download saves the song so it plays without a connection.
</Tip>

## Keep the music going

Autoplay is on by default. When the queue ends, the app adds similar songs and keeps playing.

To add a song yourself, **long-press any song row** and choose **Play next** or **Add to queue**.

## Download a song

Tap **⭳** in the player, or long-press a row and choose **Download**.

The first time, Android asks for storage permission. After that:

- The button turns into a **green tick** when the file is saved.
- The song plays without a connection and shows an **Offline** label.
- The file is a normal audio file in your music folder. Other apps can play it.

See **[Downloads & Offline](/guide/downloads)**.

## Change the sound

Open the menu: **swipe in from the left edge of Home**, or tap the menu button.

- **Equalizer**: eight bands and eleven presets. Start a song first, because it needs something playing.
- **Your activity**: your top artists, top songs and plays this week.
- **Settings → Crossfade**: opens the Playback screen with Autoplay, Normalize volume, Crossfade and the sleep timer.
- **Settings → Streaming quality**: how much data each song uses.

The sleep timer is also in the full player: the timer button at the bottom left.

<Note>
  Dragging any equalizer band switches you to **Custom**, starting from the curve you had.
</Note>

## Import from Spotify

Paste a public Spotify playlist or album link into the search box. Each song is found on the three sources and saved as a normal playlist you can edit.

See **[Import from Spotify](/guide/spotify-import)**.
