# Finding Music

One search box, three sources. This page explains what happens between typing and seeing results.

## The three sources

| Source | Best for | What to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **JioSaavn** | Studio releases, Indian music | Up to 320 kbps, with album covers and full details |
| **SoundCloud** | Remixes, uploads, independent artists | Usually 128 kbps. Preview-only or region-locked uploads are skipped |
| **YouTube** | Songs the other two do not have | Slower to start, and more likely to be a live or cover version |

All three are searched **at the same time**. The results come back as one list.

## One song, one row

If a song is on all three sources, you see it once. The other two are kept as backups on that row.

This means:

- The list is shorter and easier to read.
- If a stream fails, the app **switches source**. Usually you will not notice.
- The **source label** shows where the song is playing from right now. This can change between plays.

## How results are ranked

The app reads your search to decide which version you want. Live, cover, karaoke, instrumental, remix, nightcore and "slowed + reverb" versions are **ranked lower unless you ask for them**.

Search *blinding lights* and you get the studio version. Search *blinding lights slowed reverb* and you get that.

Also:

- Title and artist are matched loosely, so a missing apostrophe or a different spelling still works.
- A good match on both title and artist beats a perfect match on only one.
- Each source has a time limit. A slow source is **left out of the results** instead of holding up the search.

<Note>
  Because of that time limit, a search can sometimes return fewer results. Search again and the missing ones usually appear.
</Note>

## Suggestions and history

Suggestions appear as you type, and the box remembers past searches. To clear history, use **Settings → Clear cached data**. This also removes saved lyrics and temporary files. It does not touch downloads, playlists or likes.

## Browsing

**Home** shows rows of popular music and music based on what you play: albums, playlists and songs. Tap a card to open it. Play, Shuffle and long-press work the same as anywhere else.

The **genre tiles** on Search work the same way.

## When a song will not play

The app skips it and tells you:

> Skipped "Song Title" — unavailable

Every source it had for that song failed. Common reasons: a preview-only SoundCloud upload, a region-locked song, or a dead link. Search for the song again and pick a row with a different source label.

## Pasting links

The search box also accepts links:

- A **Spotify** playlist, album or song link. See [Import from Spotify](/guide/spotify-import).
- A **SoundCloud** or **YouTube** link. It plays directly.
