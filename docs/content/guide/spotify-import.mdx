# Import from Spotify

Paste a public Spotify **playlist**, **album** or **song** link into the search box. The app reads the song list, finds each song on its three sources, and saves the result as a normal playlist.

## How to

1. In Spotify, share the playlist and copy its link.
2. In Relaxify, tap **Search** and paste the link.
3. The import screen opens and goes through the list.
4. Tap **Save** to keep it as a playlist.

You do not need to sign in to anything.

## What happens

Spotify's public page lists the songs: titles, artists and albums. The app reads that list, searches its own sources for each song, and keeps the best match.

<Note>
  The app only opens a page that is already public. No Spotify account is used, and no music comes from Spotify. The audio always comes from JioSaavn, SoundCloud or YouTube.
</Note>

## What you get

A **normal playlist**, the same as one you made yourself:

- Rename it, reorder it, add and remove songs.
- Play, shuffle or download all of it.
- It does not change when the Spotify playlist changes, and it stays if that playlist is deleted.

## When a song does not match

Most songs match. Some do not:

| Case | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Only in some countries** | Not on any of the three sources. Skipped and counted in the summary |
| **Very new release** | May not be on the sources yet. Try again in a few days |
| **Remix or edit** | The closest version is used, which may be the original |
| **Live or acoustic** | Matched if the title says so. Otherwise the studio version is used |

The summary at the end shows how many songs matched and how many did not.

<Tip>
  Big playlists take a while, because each song is a search. The import keeps going if you leave the screen and come back.
</Tip>

## Other links

The search box also takes a **SoundCloud** or **YouTube** link and plays it directly, without the import screen.
