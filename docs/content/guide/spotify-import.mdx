# Spotify Import

Paste a public Spotify **playlist**, **album** or **track** link into the search box. The app reads the track list, finds each song across its own three sources, and saves the result as an ordinary playlist you own.

## How to

1. In Spotify, share the playlist and copy its link.
2. In Fix_Music, tap **Search** and paste it.
3. The import screen opens and works through the list.
4. Tap **Save** to keep it as a playlist.

That is it. There is nothing to log into.

## What actually happens

Spotify's public page carries the track list — titles, artists, album names. The app reads exactly that, then searches its own sources for each entry and keeps the best match.

<Note>
  Nothing is sent to Spotify beyond fetching a page that is already public, and no account of yours is involved at either end. Nothing streams *from* Spotify — the audio always comes from JioSaavn, SoundCloud or YouTube.
</Note>

## What you get

An **ordinary playlist**. Not a linked or synced one — a playlist exactly like one you made by hand:

- Rename it, reorder it, add and remove songs.
- Play, shuffle or download the whole thing.
- It does not update when the Spotify original changes, and it does not vanish if that original is deleted.

That independence is the point. A synced copy is a copy that can be taken away.

## Matching, and where it goes wrong

Most tracks match cleanly. Some do not, and the honest reasons are:

| Case | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Regional exclusive** | Not on any of the three sources. Skipped, and counted in the summary |
| **Very new release** | May not have propagated yet. Try again in a few days |
| **Remix or edit** | The closest available version is taken, which may be the original |
| **Live or acoustic version** | Matched if the title says so; otherwise the studio version wins |

The summary at the end says how many matched and how many did not, so a partial import is visibly partial rather than quietly wrong.

<Tip>
  Very large playlists take a while — every track is a search. The screen keeps working if you navigate away and come back.
</Tip>

## Other links

The search box also takes a direct **SoundCloud** or **YouTube** URL and resolves it straight to a playable track, no import screen involved.
