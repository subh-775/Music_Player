# Lyrics

Open the full player and tap **Lyrics** on the switch in the middle of the time row. The lyrics replace the cover.

## Timed and plain

You get one of two kinds:

- **Timed**: the current line is highlighted and the lyrics scroll with the song. Most well-known songs have these.
- **Plain**: the full text, without highlighting.

The app asks for timed lyrics first and falls back to plain ones.

## When Lyrics is dimmed

**Lyrics** is dimmed when the song has none. The app checked, and the lyrics service has nothing for it. Tap it and it tells you so.

This is common for SoundCloud and YouTube uploads, whose song details often do not match a known release.

## When they load

The app checks for lyrics **when you open the player**, so the switch can show whether lyrics exist before you tap it. With the player closed, it does not check.

<Note>
  Lyrics are saved after the first time, so replaying a song does not load them again. **Settings → Clear cached data** removes them.
</Note>

## Accuracy

Lyrics come from a public lyrics service and are matched by title, artist and length. The length tells the album version apart from a radio edit or an extended mix.

If the lyrics drift out of time or belong to another version, the song's own details are usually wrong.
