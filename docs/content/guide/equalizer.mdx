# Equalizer

Eight bands from 60 Hz to 16 kHz, from −12 to +12 dB, with eleven presets and a custom curve. Open it from the menu, or from **Settings → Equalizer**.

## Dragging a band switches to Custom

Custom **starts from the curve you had**. Pick Rock, move the 60 Hz band, and you get Rock with your change, not a flat curve.

## The bands come from your phone

Android does not let apps choose band frequencies.

Most phones have **five** bands, at frequencies the maker chose. Some have ten. The gain range differs too, for example ±12 dB on one phone and ±15 dB on another.

So the eight bands you set are **mapped onto the bands your phone has**, and kept inside its range. The mapping follows how we hear pitch, not raw Hz, so your phone's bands spread evenly across the curve.

<Note>
  The line under the switch says what the app found, for example "Shaping 5 hardware bands from these eight."
</Note>

## Why ±12 dB

More than about +12 dB does not make the bass louder. It makes the sound distort.

## Presets

| Preset | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| **Flat** | No change |
| **Rock** | More lows and highs, fewer low-mids |
| **Metal** | Like Rock, but stronger |
| **Pop** | Mids forward, the rest gentle |
| **Hip-Hop** | Heavy bass, relaxed mids |
| **Electronic** | More sub-bass and treble, fewer mids |
| **Classical** | Wide and gentle |
| **Jazz** | Warm lows, a small dip in the upper mids |
| **Vocal** | Voices forward, the rest pulled back |
| **Bass Boost** | Low end only, flat from 1 kHz |
| **Treble Boost** | High end only |
| **Custom** | Your own curve |

Tapping a preset **turns the equalizer on**.

## "Play something first"

The equalizer attaches to the sound that is playing. With nothing playing, it has nothing to work on and says so.

Start a song, then open the equalizer.

<Warn title="If it says your phone refused the effects">
  Some phones block audio effects on certain audio paths. The app cannot get around this. It shows your phone's own reason, so you can tell this apart from "play something first".
</Warn>

## Normalize volume

On the Playback screen (**Settings → Crossfade**), **Normalize volume** plays every song at about the same loudness, so a quiet old recording and a loud new one sit at a similar level.

It works on the song that is playing right away, and does nothing at all when it is off.

<Note>
  Opening the equalizer while music plays can cause one short jump in volume. The app has to attach an effect to read your phone's bands, and Android reroutes the audio when it does.
</Note>
