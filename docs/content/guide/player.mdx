# The Player

Tap the mini player to open the full player. Drag it down to close it.

## What is on screen

| Where | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Top strip** | The album you are playing from, or "Now playing". The arrow closes the player, and so does dragging this strip down |
| **Cover** | Swipe sideways to change song. Double-tap either side to jump |
| **Title** | The song name |
| **Artist** | Tap to open the artist. If there are several, you pick one. Several names scroll slowly across the line |
| **Labels** | The source and the bitrate. Turn them off in **Settings → Appearance** |
| **⭳ +** | Download, and save. The download button turns into a green tick once the song is saved. **+** adds to Liked Songs on the first press. Press again, or hold it, to add to playlists |
| **Output** | Shown in green under the buttons when headphones or a speaker are connected |
| **Song / Lyrics** | The switch in the middle of the time row. Tap to swap the cover for the lyrics. It is dimmed when a song has no lyrics |
| **Bottom row** | Speed and sleep timer on the left, the queue on the right |

## Playback speed

The speed button is in the bottom row, left of the sleep timer. Choose **1.0, 1.25, 1.5 or 2.0**, or use the slider for anything from **0.25x to 2.0x**. **+** and **−** change it by 0.05.

The pitch stays the same, so faster does not sound higher.

The speed **stays for the next songs** until you change it. When it is not 1.0x, the button shows the number.

<Note>
  Crossfade runs at the same speed, so the two songs stay in time. The sleep timer's **end of track** option counts real time: at 1.5x, a minute of music ends in 40 seconds.
</Note>

## The seek bar

Drag to move through the song, or tap to jump to a spot.

- **The song jumps when you let go**, not while you drag. This keeps dragging smooth on a slow connection.
- **The touch area is 44 pixels tall**, so the thin bar is easy to hit. A drag that goes clearly up or down closes the player instead.

## Controls

**Shuffle** turns on and off. On mixes the songs still to come. Off puts them back in their original order.

**Previous** restarts the song first. It goes to the previous song only in the first 3 seconds.

**Repeat** has two states: off, or repeat this song. With repeat on, the song plays again when it ends. Next still skips.

## Lyrics

The **Song / Lyrics** switch swaps the cover for the lyrics. Tap it to go back.

It is **dimmed** when the song has no lyrics, which is common on SoundCloud and YouTube uploads. Tapping it tells you so. See [Lyrics](/guide/lyrics).

## The bottom row

**The queue**: tap the queue button on the right, or pull up anywhere on the row. The queue starts opening as soon as you pull. Push back down to cancel. Only the button opens it with a tap.

A drag down on this row still closes the player.

**The sleep timer**: stop after 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or at the end of this song. The button turns green while a timer is set. See [Settings](/reference/settings#sleep-timer).

## Notification and lock screen

While music plays, Relaxify shows a notification with play, pause, skip and a seek bar. The same controls work on the lock screen, Bluetooth headphones and car systems.

- The notification is what lets music keep playing after you leave the app. Android needs it for background audio.
- A headset's **previous** button restarts the song first, like the button in the app.

<Note>
  Swiping the app away from Recents stops the music and removes the notification.
</Note>

## Resume

When you open the app again, it shows the song and position you left, **paused**, with your queue.
