# The Queue

Tap the queue button at the bottom right of the player, or pull up on the bottom row.

This is the player's **real** queue, not a copy, so it always matches what plays next.

## What you see

- **The current song stays at the top** while you scroll.
- The subtitle shows the artist, and where the sound is going if something is connected.
- The heading says **Next up**, or **Shuffling from** when shuffle is on.
- Songs marked **Recommended** were added by Autoplay, not by you.

## Moving songs

**Touch the ≡ handle** on any song that is coming up, and drag it. The song lifts the moment you touch the handle, and stays under your finger. To scroll the list, drag anywhere else on the rows.

Drag a song to the top or bottom edge of the list and the list scrolls with it.

<Note title="Why the playing song has no handle">
  Moving the song that is playing would stop the music. So it has no handle, and nothing can be dropped above it.
</Note>

Drag down on the sheet and the list scrolls to the top first. Then the sheet follows your finger.

A move shows right away. If the player cannot make the move, the list goes back to the real order.

## Jumping

**Tap any song** to play it. The songs before it stay in the queue.

## Adding songs

**Long-press any song row**, anywhere in the app:

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Play next** | Goes right after the current song |
| **Add to queue** | Goes at the end |

If you choose **Play next** several times in a row, those songs keep the order you added them in.

## Shuffle

Shuffle mixes every song after the current one. Turn it **off** to get the original order back. Pressing it again does not reshuffle.

## Autoplay

With Autoplay on (the default), similar songs are added when the queue runs low, so the music does not stop at the end of a playlist.

This works with the screen off too.

To let the queue end, turn off **Autoplay** on the Playback screen (**Settings → Crossfade**).
