# Gestures

Relaxify is made to use with one hand. Every gesture below also has a button. The gestures are just faster.

<GestureGrid />

## More gestures

| Gesture | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Swipe sideways** | Mini player | Next or previous song. Let go early and nothing changes |
| **Tap** | Mini player | Opens the full player |
| **Drag up** | Mini player | Opens the full player as you drag |
| **Long-press** | Any song row | Play next, add to queue, add to playlist, go to artist or album, download |
| **Drag down** | Any sheet | Closes it. If the sheet has a list, the list scrolls to the top first. Tapping outside the sheet also closes it |
| **Drag** | Equalizer band | Sets that band, in steps of 1 dB |
| **Drag or tap** | Crossfade bar | Sets the crossfade, in steps of 1 second |
| **Hold, then drag** | Queue handle | Moves the song |
| **Back** | Anywhere | Closes whatever is on top, one layer at a time |

## Swipe or double-tap on the cover

The cover takes both a swipe and a double-tap. The app tells them apart by how your finger moves:

- Moving clearly **sideways** is a swipe.
- Moving clearly **down** closes the player.
- **Not moving** is a tap, so double-tap works.

The mini player works the same way. 14 pixels sideways is a skip, 14 pixels up opens the player, and a diagonal drag goes with the bigger direction.

A swipe needs a firm push. Let go early and the cover returns, so a messy double-tap does not skip by mistake.

<Tip title="Jump further">
  Keep tapping the same side: 10, 20, 30 seconds. After the first double-tap, single taps keep adding.
</Tip>

## Back

Back closes one layer per press, starting with the one on top:

Queue → player → sheet → screen → tab → Home.

On Home with nothing open, the first press shows a warning. Press again within two seconds to exit. A stray press does not stop your music.

## The left edge

Android uses the outer edge of the screen for its own back gesture and takes those touches first.

Relaxify asks Android for a 28-pixel strip on the left edge of Home. That strip is where the menu swipe works.

<Note>
  With three-button navigation there is no conflict. The menu button at the top of Home always works too.
</Note>
