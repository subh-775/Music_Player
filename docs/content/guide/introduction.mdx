# Introduction

Relaxify is a music player for Android. It searches three music sources at once, plays from whichever one has the song, and saves songs to your phone when you ask it to.

## What it does differently

Most players use one catalogue. If that catalogue does not have a song, neither do you.

Relaxify searches **JioSaavn, SoundCloud and YouTube** together and shows the results as one list. A song found on more than one source appears once. The other sources are kept as backups on the same row.

Because of this:

- If a song fails to play, the app **switches to another source** instead of showing an error.
- A song you cannot find on one source is often on another.
- The **quality label** shows the bitrate the source actually sent, not the one you picked in Settings.

## The gestures

Swipe the cover to change song. Double-tap either side to jump 10 seconds. Drag the player down to close it. Pull up from the bottom of the player to see the queue. Swipe in from the left edge of Home to open the menu.

Every gesture also has a button.

## It runs on your phone

The search and download engine runs **on your phone**, not on a server. There is no account and nothing to sign in to. Your playlists, likes and history are saved on the phone.

Searches are only as fast as your connection to the three sources. If a source is down, its results are missing until it is back. See **[Data & Storage](/reference/architecture)**.

## What it is not

- **Not on the Play Store.** You install it from GitHub. Android asks one extra question the first time.
- **Not on iPhone.** The playback engine, audio effects and download folder use Android features that iOS does not have.
- **Not a way to avoid paying artists.** See **[Fair Use](/fair-use)**.
