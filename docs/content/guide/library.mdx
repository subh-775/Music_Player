# Your Library

Everything you keep: liked songs, playlists, albums, followed artists and downloads. It is the third tab in the bottom bar.

## Filters and pins

At the top: **All**, **Playlists**, **Albums** and **Artists**.

Long-press any row to **pin** it. Pinned rows come first. A playlist and an album with the same name are pinned separately.

Below the pins, rows are sorted by **what you changed last**. Add a song to a playlist, rename it or change its cover, and it moves up. Liked Songs and Downloads always stay in place.

Inside a playlist, the song you added last is at the **top**.

<Note>
  Followed artists do not change, so they sit at the end, in the order you followed them.
</Note>

## What is here

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Liked Songs** | Every song you liked, newest first |
| **Downloads** | Songs saved on your phone. They play without a connection |
| **Your playlists** | Made by you, or imported from Spotify |
| **Albums** | Albums you opened from a song or from Home |
| **Artists** | Artists you followed from their page |

## Playlists

Create one from the Library tab, or from **+** in the player, or from **Add to playlist** in a song's **⋮** menu. The last two create the playlist and add the song in one step.

In a playlist you can play, shuffle, reorder, rename, remove songs and delete it. Deleting a playlist does not delete downloaded songs.

## Liking

The ♥ is on song rows and in the **⋮** menu. In the player and mini player, **+** does the same job and fills in once the song is saved.

Liking a song adds it to Liked Songs and nothing else. It does not download it.

## The green marker

While music plays, the collection you **started it from** shows a green marker. A song can be in Liked Songs, a playlist and Downloads at once. The marker shows the one you started from.

## Followed artists

Follow an artist from their page and they appear here. Tapping the row opens their page: top songs, albums and a follow button.

## Downloads in the library

The Downloads list is built by **reading your download folder**. Delete a file in a file manager and it leaves the list on the next scan. Add a file by hand and it appears.

See **[Downloads & Offline](/guide/downloads)**.
