# Troubleshooting

Common problems, why they happen, and what to do.

## Playback

### A song skipped by itself

Every source the app had for that song failed: a preview-only SoundCloud upload, a region-locked song, or a dead link. The app tells you which song it skipped.

Search for the song again and pick a row with a **different source label**.

### Music stops when I leave the app

Your phone's battery saver closed the app.

Open **Settings → Apps → Relaxify → Battery** on your phone and choose **Unrestricted**. The name differs by phone: "Don't optimise", "No restrictions" or "Allow background activity".

### Music kept playing after I swiped the app away

Update to **v1.0.11** or newer.

### The volume jumps when a song starts

Fixed in **v1.0.9**. Opening the **Equalizer** while a song plays can still cause one small jump, because the app has to attach an effect to read your phone's bands.

### Crossfade only works while the app is open

Update to **v1.2.9** or newer. Crossfade now works with the screen off and with other apps open. See [Sound & Quality](/reference/sound#crossfade).

If it still does not fade, check that:

- **Crossfade** is above 0 (**Settings → Crossfade**).
- You are not pressing **next**. Skipping by hand never fades.
- **Repeat this song** and the **end of track** sleep timer are off.

### Every song says 320 kbps

Update to **v1.2.9** or newer. The label now shows what the source really sent. See [Sound & Quality](/reference/sound#streaming-quality).

## Sound

### The equalizer says to play something first

The equalizer attaches to the sound that is playing. Start a song, then open it.

### It says my phone refused the effects

Some phones block audio effects on certain audio paths. The app cannot get around this. It shows your phone's own reason.

### The equalizer bands do not move

Check that the equalizer is **switched on**. While it is off, dragging a band scrolls the page instead.

## Search and downloads

### Search is slow or shows fewer results

Each source has a time limit. A slow source is left out rather than holding up the search. Search again and the missing results usually appear.

No results at all almost always means no connection. Search needs a connection. Downloads do not.

### A downloaded song does not show as downloaded

Downloads are matched by **title and artist**. If you changed those tags, the match is lost. Download the song again.

### Downloads fail

Storage permission was turned off, or the download folder is on a card that is no longer there. Check **Settings → Download location**, and allow storage again if Android turned it off.

## Speed and battery

### The app uses battery when nothing is playing

Update to **v1.2.2** or newer.

A paused song still keeps the music notification and a small background service. That is normal. A song that is *playing* while you are not listening is not. Check the notification.

### The app starts slowly

Update to **v1.2.2** or newer. Older versions got slower to start the more you used them.

### Covers load slowly on a slow connection

Update to **v1.2.3** or newer. Covers now load at the size they are shown, and are saved after the first time.

## App

### The menu does not open when I swipe

The swipe area is the outer 28 pixels of the **left edge, on Home only**. Android also uses that edge for its back gesture.

With three-button navigation there is no conflict. The menu button at the top of Home always works.

### A song has no lyrics

The **Lyrics** switch is dimmed when a song has none. This is common for SoundCloud and YouTube uploads. See [Lyrics](/guide/lyrics).

### "The music engine stopped" when I open the app

Update to **v1.2.3** or newer. Older versions could show this during a normal start.

If it shows while you are using the app, your phone closed the engine to save battery. Close the app fully, open it again, and set its battery use to **Unrestricted**.

### Turning shuffle off does not restore the order

Update to **v1.2.4** or newer.

### I turned Autoplay off and it kept playing

Update to **v1.2.4** or newer. Turning Autoplay off now also removes the suggested songs already in the queue. Songs you added yourself stay.

### Back closes the wrong thing

Back should close whatever is on top, one layer per press. If it does not, please report it with the exact screens you went through.

## Updates

### The update dot never appears

Update to **v1.0.10** or newer by hand once. After that, the app checks on start and each time you come back to it.

### I updated and lost my playlists

The app was uninstalled before the new version was installed. Android deletes app data on uninstall, and it cannot be recovered.

Always install the new version **over** the old one. The in-app updater does this. Downloads are safe either way.

## Still stuck

**[Open an issue](https://github.com/subh-775/Music_Player/issues)** with what you did, what happened, your Android version and your phone model.
